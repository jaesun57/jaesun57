### Research

- **Kinetic MHD stability of negative-triangularity plasmas** — ideal, drift-kinetic, and full kinetic MHD models with the GPEC suite; kinetic stabilization, trapped-particle resonance, and the second-stability region of low-*n* modes in DIII-D NT plasmas.
- **Nonlinear 3D MHD and pilot-plant design** — transport analysis and edge-stability modeling for NSTX-U and KSTAR plasmas, including BALOO for NT and K-DEMO studies.
- **Data infrastructure and high-performance computing** — IMAS-HSDS database architecture for tokamak experiments, automated diagnostic post-processing, and GPU-accelerated numerics.

Currently an undergraduate research intern in the [3D Stellarator & Tokamak Lab](https://nucleng.snu.ac.kr/about/people/faculty?mode=view&profidx=167) at Seoul National University under Prof. Jong-Kyu Park. Previously at the **Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory** (SNU–PPPL Summer Collaboration, 2025) and SNU's [Plasma & Ion Beam Laboratory](https://nucleng.snu.ac.kr/about/people/faculty?mode=view&profidx=169) under Prof. Y.
